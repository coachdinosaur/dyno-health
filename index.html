Urgent care guide

Red flags that should not wait

Blood pressure

Above 180/120 mm Hg with chest pain, shortness of breath, weakness, numbness, vision change, or trouble speaking: call emergency services. Without symptoms, repeat after at least one minute and contact a health professional promptly if still that high.

Diabetes

Severe low blood sugar, unconsciousness, seizure, inability to swallow, high ketones, fruity breath, deep breathing, repeated vomiting, or inability to keep fluids down needs immediate help.

Skin

Fever with rapidly spreading redness, warmth, swelling, pus, yellow crusts, severe pain, or clusters of painful blisters can signal infection and needs urgent assessment.

Joints

A suddenly very painful, hot, swollen joint with fever, chills, or feeling very unwell can be a joint infection and needs urgent medical assessment.
